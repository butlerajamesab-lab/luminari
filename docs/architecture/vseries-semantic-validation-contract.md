Status: design proposal for review; not a promoted policy, executable validator, semantic certificate or production connection. Date: November 16, 2026 (Pacific).

This is one bounded continuation: recover semantic responsibilities from the supplied historical material, map them to the retained continuity matrix, and define evidence and acceptance requirements for a V-series implementation. V14, V15 and V16 assignments remain open.

## Responsibility and entry point

Docket/source preparation supplies acquisition, identity, extraction and convention evidence at its existing boundary. Rosetta owns legal decomposition and the proposed semantic observation. Independent verification and the consumer admission/publication boundary must verify the corresponding evidence. Lighthouse must consume Rosetta-owned legal evidence without independently changing its meaning.

Every eligible completed V-series decomposition, regardless of ingestion route, must be considered by the semantic entry point once this policy is implemented and promoted. This is universal routing, not thirty filter executions or thirty pass receipts per document. Individual checks execute only where their reviewed applicability rules require them. A changed semantic validator alone must not refetch provider bytes or rerun source acquisition.

The semantic observation is separate from the immutable producer output. It can identify defects and hold downstream eligibility; it cannot repair the original output in place. Any remedy needs an exact failure class, a separately proven bounded action, a successor identity and independent revalidation.

## Evidence identity and semantic representation

A V-series semantic observation must bind to all of the following:

- Exact source_content_id and source_content_hash.
- Producer ruleset_version and ruleset_sha256 resolved through the selected runtime contract.
- Exact output_sha256 and its declared digest contract. Bind any additional parentage, operative projection or markup evidence not covered by that digest separately.
- Semantic contract, validator implementation fingerprint and promoted check/applicability manifest fingerprints.
- Stable unit references, evidence hashes, and an invocation/completion identity owned by the authorized validator. Execution-attempt identity is additional evidence; a historical extraction_run integer is not the V-series output identity.

Caller-supplied JSON with a pass flag is not an authenticated receipt. The eventual storage/admission implementation must enforce receipt ownership and identity at the shared database boundary, including direct-call paths. This document adds no table or privilege.

The semantic representation may describe actor, modality/polarity, action/object, beneficiary, condition, deadline, exception, definition scope, parent context and unresolved relationships. These are auxiliary evidence fields; their introduction does not replace H/W/A/O/D or promote the proposed R layer. A field absent from a legal statement need not be invented.

Every asserted field needs exact source evidence. Inherited actors, list lead-ins and definition context need references to the governing parent and an explicit scope exit. Temporal/conditional text must remain associated with the rule even when it is separated from the actor. Multiple modal words alone do not prove a legal contradiction.

Raw source text, deletion-masked display text and internal operative classification text are distinct representations. A semantic adapter must declare which it used, preserve original code-point positions, and consume preparation/markup evidence. It must not reintroduce deleted text, summaries or page furniture by blindly reparsing the raw display span. Original-byte coordinate proof remains a separate upstream obligation.

## Applicability, coverage and outcomes

The promoted check manifest must identify each check's scope and deterministic applicability rule. A proven not-applicable decision records its basis without running the filter or requiring a pass receipt. Unknown applicability is pending at the affected scope. Missing or failed upstream evidence cannot be silently converted to a skip.

For an applicable check, a missing completion/result is pending; a failed result is blocked; a passing result is usable only for its exact bound identities and declared scope. Missing named checks never default to passed. Completion of a worker attempt is distinct from validation passing, semantic certification and publication authorization.

Coverage must account for every operative producer unit and any additional operative source statements found independently: checked, supported by explicit parent evidence, outside the proven grammar/unresolved, or excluded with source-based justification. An empty candidate list is not evidence that a document has no legal statements. Non-modal provisions, including eligibility, status and appropriations text, must remain visible when the validator cannot establish their meaning.

The validator must compare against independent source evidence, rather than treating the producer's own labels as its answer key. Parser self-consistency and synthetic tests are not independent legal certification. Promotion requires independently reviewed legal labels and source boundaries for the claimed failure class.

## Consumer and publication boundary

Rosetta inspection, semantic validation, reader projection, Genome eligibility and publication are separate decisions. Held or unresolved material remains inspectable as evidence. Inputs needed to perform verification must not depend on a publication gate that requires verification already to have finished.

Consumer admission must recheck the exact source, producer output, current semantic policy and certification identity. An older parser result, another output's pass, a revoked result or a newer failed result cannot silently authorize current output or revive an older current-law display. Historical evidence remains separately identifiable under the platform's governed retention policy.

An aggregate pass is limited to the promoted check set and proven grammar coverage. Reader and Genome eligibility require their own contract checks. A deterministic plain-language template is not a factual application or legal conclusion. Neither an integrity receipt nor this proposed semantic observation authorizes publication by itself.

One document's semantic failure must not globally stop unrelated work. Execution errors, semantic failures and unknown applicability need distinct observations. No automatic historical backfill, retries or remedy loop is authorized by this proposal; worker scope and bounded replay policy require their own reviewed acceptance.

## Acceptance required before connecting an implementation

The current six sources / 72 units are diagnostic specimens, not independent gold. Preserve their original source/output hashes and compare any successor by exact affected spans, hierarchy, semantic claims and unchanged controls. Reconcile and independently review any draft gold archive before using it as a promotion authority.

The minimum future acceptance cases are:

1. Applicable missing/failed checks prevent admission; proven irrelevant checks do not execute or need pass receipts; unknown applicability remains pending.
2. Wrong source, producer generation, output digest, validator/check manifest or completion identity cannot bind. Changed validator identity cannot reuse an older receipt as current acceptance.
3. Empty, unsupported and partially recognized operative content cannot obtain whole-document acceptance through vacuous success. All operative content is accounted for.
4. Source preparation/deletion evidence, nested parentage, list exits, full definition occurrences, negation and qualifiers survive the handoff. Misleading lexical cues are paired with opposite controls.
5. A completed attempt with failed validation stays blocked. Interrupted writes cannot create a usable completion. Identical governed replay preserves evidence; drift creates a distinct identity or rejection.
6. Reader/Genome/publication gates consume authenticated current evidence through every supported entry path. Direct database calls cannot bypass the same admission requirement.
7. A newer failure, stale result, mismatched source/output, or effective revocation cannot restore older output as current law. Inspection and verification inputs remain available without publication.
8. Independent legal labels, protected unaffected controls, bounded affected-class deltas, and preserved retired-execution guards are demonstrated in a disposable full-path acceptance run.

Open before implementation: the promoted semantic grammar/check manifest, independent legal-label authority, authentic V-series receipt storage/API, operative projection evidence, and concrete downstream admission caller. No existing historical semantic helper is promoted by renaming it or changing its engine filter.

The next bounded implementation must name one failure class, affected cohort, protected controls, source/output/validator bindings and producer/consumer acceptance. This document does not select that class or allocate a V14-V16 number.
